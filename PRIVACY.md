# Privacy policy

afterm will not transfer any information to other networked systems unless specifically requested
by the user or the person installing or operating it.

## What afterm sends, and where

afterm connects only to the SSH hosts you tell it to connect to. Everything it sends over those
connections — your keystrokes, files you upload, a screenshot you paste, the commands that read the
host's CPU, memory, disk and network figures — goes to that host and nowhere else.

afterm has no telemetry, analytics, crash reporting, advertising or update check, and it has no
account. It does not contact the afterm project or any other third party. A link you `Ctrl`-click
in the terminal opens in your web browser, which is then subject to that site's own policy.

## What afterm keeps on your computer

- **Saved hosts** — the names, addresses, user names and settings you save — in afterm's settings
  folder under your Windows user profile.
- **Remembered passwords**, only for hosts where you turn this on, in Windows Credential Manager.
  afterm never shows a saved password back to you or sends it anywhere except to the host it
  belongs to.
- **Host keys** you accept, in your OpenSSH `known_hosts` file, the same file the OpenSSH client
  uses.
- **Interface preferences** such as the colour scheme and font size.

None of this leaves your computer. Uninstalling afterm removes the program; *Settings ▸ Forget all*
removes remembered passwords, and the settings folder can be deleted to remove the rest.

## Downloads and code signing

afterm is downloaded from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/afarzin1/afterm/releases), which is
subject to [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
Release signing through SignPath Foundation involves no data about users.
